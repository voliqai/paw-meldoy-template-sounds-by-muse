# 03 — HOW IT WORKS / DEMO AD (30 seconds)

**Concept:** For cold audiences who think "sounds nice, but how?" — show the 3 steps simply.
**Format:** 9:16 — 1080×1920 | **Style:** Clean, bright, instructional but warm. Big numbered steps.
**Voiceover:** Friendly, clear, slightly faster than the Hero ad.

---

## TIMELINE

| Time | Step | Visual | Voiceover (EN) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Hook | Happy dog + cat together looking at camera | *"Your pet's favorite sound in the world? Your voice."* |
| 3–10s | STEP 1 | Owner records voice on phone app | *"Step one: record ten seconds of your voice in the app."* |
| 10–18s | STEP 2 | Choose pet, choose moment, AI generates song | *"Step two: pick your pet and the moment — bedtime, home alone, storms. AI creates a personal lullaby with their name, sung in your voice."* |
| 18–25s | STEP 3 | One tap → TV at home plays → pet relaxes | *"Step three: send it to your Android TV or Fire TV at home. Press play — from anywhere."* |
| 25–30s | CTA | Pets asleep + end card | *"PawMelody. Your voice. Their calm. Try it free today."* |

**📝 Persistent element:** Big step badges `1` `2` `3` (lavender circle, white number) appear top-left at each step. Add in CapCut.

---

## SCENE 1 — Hook (0–3s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A happy golden retriever and a fluffy grey cat sitting side by side on a sofa looking directly at camera with cute head tilts, bright cozy living room, sunlight, cinematic photorealistic, joyful warm mood, close-up
```
**Camera:** Static close-up. **Duration:** 5s (use 3s)
**📝 Text:** `Your pet's favorite sound? YOUR voice 💛`

## SCENE 2 — Step 1: Record (3–10s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A smiling man in his thirties sitting on a sofa at home with a cat beside him, holding a smartphone and speaking warmly into it to record his voice, cozy living room, evening lamp light, cinematic photorealistic
```
**Camera:** Medium shot, slow push-in. **Duration:** 5s (extend in edit with the app overlay below)
**📝 Text:** `1. Record your voice 🎙️` + small: `Just 10 seconds`
**🎬 Edit:** Last 3s of this scene = REAL app screen recording full-screen (record screen: open app → tap record → waveform). This proves the app is real. Add a finger-tap animation.

## SCENE 3 — Step 2: AI Creates the Song (10–18s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
Close-up over the shoulder of a person holding a smartphone, soft golden musical notes and gentle glowing waves rising from the phone screen, a cat silhouette listening in soft focus, dreamy warm light, magical but cozy, cinematic photorealistic, slow motion sparkles
```
**Camera:** Slow orbit around phone. **Duration:** 5s
**📝 Text:** `2. AI creates their song ✨` + small: `Their name. Your voice.`
**🎬 Edit:** Intercut 3s of REAL app screens: pet selection → moment selection (Home Alone / Bedtime / Storms) → "Generating..." → song appears with pet name. Fast, clean cuts on the music beat.

## SCENE 4 — Step 3: Play on TV at Home (18–25s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt A (send, 18–21s):**
```
A person's hand holding a smartphone tapping the screen, in the blurred background a TV on the wall lights up with warm golden glow, cozy living room, cinematic photorealistic
```
**🎥 Kling Prompt B (pet payoff, 21–25s):**
```
A fluffy grey cat curled on a cushion in front of a glowing TV in a cozy living room, eyes slowly closing, tail tip moving gently, warm evening light, cinematic photorealistic, peaceful mood
```
**📝 Text:** `3. Play it on your TV 📺` + small: `Android TV & Fire TV — from anywhere`
**🎬 Edit:** On TV screen overlay the PawMelody player UI (CapCut overlay). Phone shows a big "▶ Play on Living Room TV" button tap.
**🔊 Sound:** Lullaby starts on the TV, cat purr (subtle).

## SCENE 5 — CTA (25–30s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt (background):**
```
A golden retriever and a fluffy grey cat sleeping peacefully together on a large cushion in a cozy living room, warm TV glow blurred behind them, evening light, cinematic photorealistic, empty space at top of frame
```
**📝 End card:**
```
🐾 PawMelody
Your voice. Their calm.
[ Try It Free ]
Android • Fire TV
```
**🔊 Sound:** Music resolves. End with a soft purr + dog sigh.

## NOTES
- This is your **explanation ad** — pair it with the Hero ad in the same campaign (Hero = emotion, this = logic).
- Keep text BIG: step titles at least 1/8 of screen height. People watch on phones, often outside in sunlight.
- If you have a dog AND cat audience, split this into a dog-only and cat-only version at the Advantage+ level later — start broad with this mixed version.
