# 04 — UGC-STYLE TESTIMONIAL AD (20 seconds)

**Concept:** Looks like a real owner filmed it on her phone. UGC-style ads often beat polished ads on Facebook because they feel like a friend's recommendation, not an ad.
**Format:** 9:16 — 1080×1920 | **Style:** Handheld phone look, natural light, NO cinematic look.
**⚠️ Kling tip:** For UGC, add `shot on smartphone, handheld, natural light, realistic amateur video` to every prompt and LOWER the polish. Perfect = suspicious.

---

## TIMELINE

| Time | Visual | She says (voiceover / on-camera) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Selfie-style: woman at her front door, dog behind her | *"I used to feel SO guilty leaving Max alone..."* |
| 4–10s | She shows her phone, records voice, dog watches | *"...so now I record my voice, and PawMelody turns it into a song with his name in it."* |
| 10–16s | Living room: TV playing, Max lying calm in his bed | *"I put it on the TV before I leave. Look at him. He hears me all day."* |
| 16–20s | She smiles at camera, dog asleep behind her | *"Honestly? Game changer. Try it."* |

**📝 On-screen text style:** TikTok/Reels caption style — white bold text with black outline, center-bottom, appearing word-by-word (CapCut auto-captions). Plus a small top banner from 0:04: `PawMelody app 🐾`

---

## SCENE 1 — Selfie hook (0–4s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A woman in her early thirties filming herself selfie style with a smartphone in her apartment hallway, a golden retriever sitting behind her near the front door, she looks at camera with a guilty emotional expression, shot on smartphone, handheld slight movement, natural window light, realistic amateur video, no filter
```
**Camera:** Handheld selfie, tiny natural shake. **Duration:** 5s

## SCENE 2 — Show the phone (4–10s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
Handheld smartphone video in a living room, a woman holding a phone showing it to camera then speaking softly into it, a golden retriever watching her curiously with head tilt, shot on smartphone, natural light, realistic amateur video, warm home atmosphere
```
**Camera:** Handheld, pans from her face down to phone, back up. **Duration:** 5s
**🎬 Edit:** Overlay REAL app recording screen on the phone for 2s (CapCut mask). Keep the handheld shake — don't stabilize it away.

## SCENE 3 — The proof (10–16s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
Handheld smartphone video panning across a cozy living room, a TV glowing warmly on the wall, a golden retriever lying completely relaxed in a dog bed in front of the TV with eyes half closed, shot on smartphone, natural evening light, realistic amateur video, peaceful mood
```
**Camera:** Slow handheld pan left → right, ends on dog. **Duration:** 5s
**🎬 Edit:** Overlay PawMelody player UI on the TV. Add subtle music-note sticker. Let the lullaby play audibly here — it's the "proof".

## SCENE 4 — Recommendation (16–20s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A woman filming herself selfie style on a sofa smiling warmly at camera giving a thumbs up, a golden retriever sleeping peacefully on a cushion blurred behind her, shot on smartphone, handheld, natural light, realistic amateur video, genuine happy expression
```
**Camera:** Handheld selfie. **Duration:** 5s
**📝 Text (big, final 2s):** `Try PawMelody free 🐾⬇️`

---

## VOICEOVER NOTE
Best option: a real person records this on a phone (you, a friend, a $20 Fiverr UGC creator with a dog). AI voice kills the UGC effect.
If you must use AI voice: choose the most casual/conversational voice available, add "um", keep it imperfect.

**Versions to make:** Film/record this script with 2–3 different "owners" (woman+dog, man+cat, older woman+small dog). Same script, different faces = your best retargeting pool.
