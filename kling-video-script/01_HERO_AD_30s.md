# 01 — HERO AD (30 seconds) ⭐ Start with this one

**Concept:** The guilt every pet owner feels when leaving → your voice stays home.
**Angle:** Emotional. Dog version (also works with a cat — swap the pet in the prompts).
**Format:** 9:16 vertical — 1080×1920 | **Platform:** Facebook/Instagram Reels, Stories, Feed
**Voiceover:** Warm female or male voice, 30–40 years old, soft and caring. Scripts in EN/FR/ES: see [VOICEOVER_AND_TEXT.md](VOICEOVER_AND_TEXT.md)

---

## TIMELINE OVERVIEW

| Time | Scene | What the viewer feels |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | The goodbye at the door | "That's me. I feel guilty too." |
| 4–9s | Owner records their voice | Curiosity — "What is she doing?" |
| 9–15s | AI creates the song, sends to TV | "Wow, that's clever." |
| 15–24s | Pet relaxes at home hearing owner's voice | Relief, emotion — the payoff |
| 24–30s | Logo + CTA | Click |

**Music under the whole ad:** Soft piano + acoustic guitar, slow, warm (use a PawMelody song itself as the ad music — it proves the product). Start quiet/sad, turn warm at 0:09.

---

## SCENE 1 — The Goodbye (0–4s) — THE HOOK

**Visual:** A woman kneels at her front door, bag in hand, saying goodbye to her golden retriever. The dog looks up with big eyes. She closes the door. The dog stays, watching the door.

**🎥 Kling Prompt (copy-paste):**
```
A young woman in a cozy beige sweater kneeling by a front door in a warm modern living room, gently petting a golden retriever, the dog looking up at her with soft sad eyes, natural morning light through window, she stands and opens the door to leave, emotional tender moment, cinematic photorealistic style, shallow depth of field
```
**Camera:** Slow push-in toward the dog's face. End on the dog's eyes.
**Duration:** 5s | **Mode:** Professional | **Ratio:** 9:16

**Negative prompt:**
```
distorted animal, extra legs, deformed paws, melting face, scary, text, watermark, logo, blurry, cartoon, horror
```

**🎙️ Voiceover (EN):** *"Leaving your best friend home alone... never gets easier."*
**📝 On-screen text (big, top third):** `Leaving your pet home alone? 🐾`
**🔊 Sound:** House ambience, keys, soft door click. Music: single sad piano notes.

> **Editing tip:** Add the text in CapCut, NOT in Kling. Freeze the dog's face for 0.5s at the end — it's your scroll-stopper.

---

## SCENE 2 — Record Your Voice (4–9s)

**Visual:** Same woman, now at her office / on a train, smiling at her phone. She taps record and speaks softly into it: "Hey buddy, I'm here..." Close-up on her warm smile.

**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A young woman in a beige sweater sitting in a bright modern office, smiling warmly while holding a smartphone close to her mouth, speaking softly and lovingly into the phone as if recording a voice message, laptop blurred in background, natural daylight, cinematic photorealistic, tender happy expression
```
**Camera:** Slow zoom-in from medium shot to close-up of her smiling face + phone.
**Duration:** 5s | **Mode:** Professional | **Ratio:** 9:16

**🎙️ Voiceover:** *"So I recorded my voice in PawMelody..."*
**📝 On-screen text:** `1. Record your voice 🎙️`
**🔊 Sound:** Soft "tap" sound. Music begins to warm up.

> **Editing tip:** Overlay a REAL screen recording of the PawMelody record button over the phone at the end of this scene (CapCut > Overlay > your screen recording, masked to the phone). 1 second is enough.

---

## SCENE 3 — The AI Creates His Song (9–15s)

**Visual:** Magical moment. Sound waves and soft golden musical notes float from the phone. Cut to: a TV in the living room at home lights up by itself with a warm glow.

**🎥 Kling Prompt A (phone magic, 9–12s):**
```
Extreme close-up of a smartphone on an office desk, soft golden glowing musical notes and gentle light waves floating up from the screen into the air, magical warm sparkles, dreamy atmosphere, dark soft background, cinematic photorealistic, slow motion particles
```
**Camera:** Static close-up, particles rise. **Duration:** 5s

**🎥 Kling Prompt B (TV turns on, 12–15s):**
```
A cozy living room interior, a large flat screen TV on the wall turning on with a warm soft golden glow, empty golden retriever dog bed and a sofa in front of it, afternoon light, no people, peaceful home atmosphere, cinematic photorealistic
```
**Camera:** Slow dolly forward toward the TV. **Duration:** 5s

**🎙️ Voiceover:** *"...and it turned it into a calming song — with his name, in my voice."*
**📝 On-screen text:** `2. AI creates a song with his name ✨` → then `3. It plays on your TV at home 📺`
**🔊 Sound:** Gentle chime whoosh. Now the PawMelody lullaby (with the pet's name) starts playing — this stays to the end.

> **Editing tip:** On the TV screen, overlay in CapCut: the PawMelody player screen / song cover art + the name "Buddy" + a play waveform. This is your product proof shot — make it clean and readable for 2 full seconds.

---

## SCENE 4 — He's Not Alone Anymore (15–24s) — THE PAYOFF

**Visual:** The golden retriever hears the TV. Ears up, head tilt. He walks to his bed in front of the TV, circles, lies down, sighs, and relaxes — eyes slowly closing while his owner's singing voice plays.

**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A golden retriever in a cozy living room turning its head toward a glowing TV, ears perked up with a cute head tilt, then walking slowly to a soft dog bed in front of the TV, lying down, resting its chin on its paws, eyes slowly closing in relaxation, warm golden light from the TV, cinematic photorealistic, heartwarming peaceful mood
```
**Camera:** Start on dog close-up → slow pull-back to wide shot of dog + TV.
**Duration:** 10s (generate as 10s, or 2× 5s and cut) | **Mode:** Professional

**🎙️ Voiceover:** *"Now, on my TV at home, he hears me singing to him. And he can finally relax."*
**📝 On-screen text:** `He hears YOUR voice 💛` then `Calm. Safe. Not alone.`
**🔊 Sound:** The lullaby is loudest here. Add a soft dog sigh at 0:21.

> This is the scene people will rewatch and share. If the dog's lying-down motion looks odd, regenerate — or generate with Image-to-Video using a photo of a dog lying in a bed.

## 📋 EDITING CHECKLIST (CapCut)

1. Import 6 Kling clips in scene order, trim to timeline above.
2. Speed-ramp Scene 4 lying-down moment to 0.8× (slower = calmer).
3. Voiceover track (record yourself or AI voice — settings in VOICEOVER_AND_TEXT.md).
4. Music: one PawMelody lullaby underneath, ducked −12dB under voiceover, full volume in Scene 4.
5. Subtitles: auto-captions → style: bold white, black stroke, bottom-center, large. Check they don't cover the dog's face.
6. Real app UI overlays in Scenes 2–3 (phone + TV screens).
7. End card 3s, logo centered, button with subtle pulse animation.
8. Export: 1080×1920, MP4 H.264, 30fps, ~20–30 Mbps. Name it: `PawMelody_HERO_Dog_HomeAlone_30s_9x16.mp4`
