# FACEBOOK / META ADS GUIDE 📱

How to export, write, and launch these videos so they actually perform.

---

## 1. EXPORT SPECS

| Placement | Ratio | Resolution | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reels & Stories (make this first)** | **9:16** | **1080 × 1920** | MP4, H.264, 30fps |
| Feed (repurpose) | 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 | Crop from the 9:16 master in CapCut |
| Feed square (backup) | 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 | Only if 4:5 underperforms |

- Duration: **15s or 30s** exactly as scripted. Under 31s = eligible for all placements.
- Bitrate: 20–30 Mbps export. File size under 250MB (you'll be ~30–60MB — fine).
- **First frame = thumbnail.** Before exporting, check frame 1: cute pet face + one readable hook line. If frame 1 is a door or a blur, add a 0.3s title card in CapCut.
- Upload the 9:16 master and let Meta's Advantage+ placements do the rest at launch.

### Safe zones (9:16) — keep text & faces OUT of these
```
┌─────────────────┐
│  TOP 15% — Meta UI (profile, audio icon) — NO text │
│                 │
│   ✅ SAFE AREA   │  ← pet faces, hook text, steps
│                 │
│  BOTTOM 20% — caption, CTA button, progress bar — NO key text │
└─────────────────┘
```
Your burned-in subtitles live in the lower-middle, just ABOVE the bottom 20%.

## 3. COMPLIANCE — read before launching ⚠️

Meta rejects ads with false medical claims, and PawMelody's own rules forbid them too.

| ❌ NEVER write | ✅ Write instead |
|---|---|
| cures / treats anxiety | helps your pet feel safe and calm |
| clinically proven, vet approved (unless literally true & documented) | loved by pet owners |
| reduces stress hormones | comforts your pet |
| stops barking / fixes separation anxiety | keeps them company with your voice |

Also: no before/after suffering imagery that's graphic, no "YOU are neglecting your pet" shaming hooks. Guilt is the emotion — but the ad must end warm, never accusatory. Positive framing also gets cheaper reach on Meta.

## 4. LAUNCH PLAN (first 2 weeks)

**Week 1 — Find the winner**
- 1 campaign, Advantage+ / broad targeting, dog+cat owners interest stack is optional — start BROAD (Meta's algorithm + a pet video finds pet owners fast).
- 3 creatives only: **Hero 30s**, **Short Hook 15s (Version B, no VO)**, **How It Works 30s**.
- Budget: small daily test budget per creative for 3–4 days. Kill rule: no clicks after meaningful spend = hook problem → swap hook text (Short Hook doc lists 5 ready hooks), don't rebuild the video.
- Watch: **Thumb-stop rate** (3s views / impressions — aim 30%+), **hold rate** (50% watched), CTR, cost per install.

**Week 2 — Scale & multiply**
- Winner gets 70% of budget. Generate the **cat version** of the winner + **UGC testimonial**.
- Add the **Storm ad** if a fireworks/storm moment is near.
- Retargeting: video viewers 50%+ → show UGC testimonial + "Try free" hard CTA.

**Creative refresh:** Pet ads fatigue in 2–4 weeks. Your re-roll cost is low by design: new hook text on the same clips, or one new Kling Scene 1. Keep a rotation of 3–4 live creatives.

## 6. QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Kling pet looks distorted | Professional Mode, shorten to 5s, one action only, re-roll 2–3× |
| Pet changes between scenes | Image-to-Video from a screenshot of your best clip (see copy-paste sheet) |
| TV/phone screen text is gibberish | Expected. Cover it with real app UI overlay in CapCut |
| Ad approved but no thumb-stops | New Scene 1: closer pet face, eye contact with camera, bigger hook text |
| People watch but don't click | End card: hold 3 full seconds, add pulsing button + "Free" in headline |
