## ON-SCREEN TEXT — Rules for every ad

1. **Burned-in subtitles ALWAYS** (CapCut → Text → Auto Captions). 85% watch muted.
2. **Style:** Bold sans (CapCut "System Bold" / Anton), white fill, black stroke 8–12, slight shadow. Size: readable at arm's length on a phone.
3. **Position:** Center-lower third. Never over the pet's face. Keep the top 15% and bottom 20% clear (Facebook UI covers them — see Safe Zones in the Ads Guide).
4. **Max 5 words per line, max 2 lines.** One idea at a time.
5. **Highlight the money words** in your brand accent (soft peach/lavender): `YOUR voice`, `his NAME`, `FREE`.
6. Emojis: max 1 per text card, only 🐾 🎙️ 🎵 📺 💛. They lift thumb-stop rate but look spammy in excess.
