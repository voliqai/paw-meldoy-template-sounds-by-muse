# 05 — TWO EXTRA ANGLES (15s each)

Make these after the Hero ad works. Each tests a different pain point so Meta can find different buyers.

---

# AD A — "STORM / FIREWORKS" (15s)

**Angle:** Thunderstorms & fireworks nights. Very strong around New Year, July 4th, storm season.
**Timing note:** Launch this one before fireworks holidays — highest intent of the year.

| Time | Visual | Text / Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Dog trembling under a table, lightning flash at window | `Storms terrify your pet? ⛈️` |
| 3–8s | Owner's voice song plays from TV, warm glow | `Your voice, as a calming song 🎵` |
| 8–13s | Dog in bed, breathing slows, relaxes as "thunder" fades | `Safe. Calm. With you. 💛` |
| 13–15s | End card | `PawMelody — Try It Free 🐾` |

**🎥 Kling Prompt 1 (0–3s):**
```
A small dog hiding under a wooden table in a dim living room during a thunderstorm, blue lightning flash through window, dog trembling slightly looking scared but safe, rain on window, cinematic photorealistic, dramatic soft light
```
**🎥 Kling Prompt 2 (3–8s):**
```
The same living room now warm with golden light from a TV turning on, soft musical notes glow in the air, the small dog under the table lifts its head listening, cinematic photorealistic, mood shifting from scary to warm
```
**🎥 Kling Prompt 3 (8–13s):**
```
A small dog now lying relaxed in a cozy dog bed in a warm living room, breathing slowly, eyes closing peacefully, TV warm glow, rain softly outside window but calm inside, cinematic photorealistic, heartwarming
```
**⚠️ Compliance:** Never write "cures anxiety / treats fear". Approved wording: `helps your pet feel safe`, `comforts your pet during storms`. See [FACEBOOK_ADS_GUIDE.md](FACEBOOK_ADS_GUIDE.md).

# AD B — "WORK FROM OFFICE / CAMERA MOMENT" (15s)

**Angle:** For owners who check pet cameras at work. Relatable, funny-sad, very shareable.

| Time | Visual | Text / Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Office: woman checks pet camera on phone — dog staring at door | `Checking the pet cam at work again? 🥺` |
| 3–8s | She taps "Play on TV" in the app | `One tap: your voice plays at home ▶️📺` |
| 8–13s | Split feeling: dog at home hears it, tail wags, settles in bed | `He hears you. He settles. 💛` |
| 13–15s | End card | `PawMelody — Try It Free 🐾` |

**🎥 Kling Prompt 1 (0–3s):**
```
A woman at an office desk looking worried at her smartphone, blurred office background with colleagues, she bites her lip with a guilty expression, natural office light, cinematic photorealistic
```
*Edit: overlay a real "pet cam" style frame on her phone in CapCut (dog at door, timestamp corner) — this gag makes the ad.*
**🎥 Kling Prompt 2 (3–8s):**
```
Close-up of hands holding a smartphone tapping a glowing play button, warm light reflecting on fingers, soft office background blur, cinematic photorealistic
```
**🎥 Kling Prompt 3 (8–13s):**
```
A dog at home hearing something, ears up, happy tail wag, walking to its bed in front of a warm glowing TV and lying down relaxed, cozy living room, cinematic photorealistic, heartwarming
```

## ALSO WORTH TESTING LATER (one-line concepts)
- **Bedtime:** `A lullaby with their name, in your voice, every night.` — puppy/cat falling asleep to owner's voice.
- **New home / adoption:** `New house. Strange sounds. Your voice makes it home.` — first-evening-home moment.
- **Senior pet:** `For the old friend who has loved you longest.` — grey-muzzled dog, very emotional, high shares.
- **Vet / car ride:** `The car ride doesn't have to be the scary part.` — pet calm in car with owner's song.
