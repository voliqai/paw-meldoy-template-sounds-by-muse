# 02 — SHORT HOOK AD (15 seconds)

**Concept:** One pain, one wow, one click. Built for volume testing on Reels/Stories.
**Format:** 9:16 — 1080×1920 | **Voiceover:** Optional — this ad must work 100% muted with text only.

---

## TIMELINE

| Time | Scene | On-Screen Text (big & bold) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Dog alone at the door | `You leave. He waits... all day. 😢` |
| 3–7s | Owner records voice on phone | `Record your voice 🎙️` |
| 7–11s | TV plays at home, dog relaxes | `Your voice becomes his song 🎵📺` |
| 11–15s | Sleeping dog + end card | `PawMelody — Try It Free 🐾` |

---

## SCENE 1 — Hook (0–3s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A sad golden retriever sitting alone in front of a closed front door in a quiet living room, looking at the door, chin low, big expressive eyes looking at camera, soft grey morning light, cinematic photorealistic, emotional, close-up on the dog face
```
**Camera:** Static close-up, dog looks into camera at the end. **Duration:** 5s (use first 3s)
**🔊 Sound:** Silence + clock ticking. Sad piano, 2 notes.
**⚠️ The dog looking AT the camera is the scroll-stopper. Regenerate until you get eye contact.**

## SCENE 2 — Record (3–7s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
Close-up of a woman's hands holding a smartphone, her smiling face softly blurred behind it, she taps the screen and speaks warmly into the phone, bright cafe light, cinematic photorealistic, warm and happy mood
```
**Camera:** Slow push-in. **Duration:** 5s
**📝 Text animation:** Words pop one by one: `Record` → `your` → `voice` 🎙️
**🔊 Sound:** Upbeat turn — warm guitar joins the piano.

## SCENE 3 — Payoff (7–11s)
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A golden retriever lying relaxed in a dog bed facing a glowing TV in a cozy living room, tail slowly wagging, ears relaxed, warm golden evening light, cinematic photorealistic, peaceful heartwarming mood
```
**Camera:** Slow pull-back. **Duration:** 5s
**📝 Text:** `Your voice becomes his song 🎵` — add a music-note animation sticker in CapCut.
**🔊 Sound:** PawMelody lullaby plays (with pet name "Buddy" sung). Dog sigh.

## SCENE 4 — CTA (11–15s)
**Reuse** the sleeping-dog clip from [01_HERO_AD_30s.md](01_HERO_AD_30s.md) Scene 5, or generate:
**🎥 Kling Prompt:**
```
A golden retriever sleeping peacefully in a soft bed, cozy living room, TV warm glow in blurred background, evening light, cinematic photorealistic, empty space at top of frame
```
**📝 End card (CapCut):**
```
🐾 PawMelody
Your voice. Their calm.
[ Try It Free ]
```
**🔊 Sound:** Music resolves + soft "pop" on CTA.
