# My Voice — Home Video Script 🎙️🐾

**Film this at home. Just your phone. No Kling, no camera crew, no actors.**
**Length:** 35 seconds | **Format:** Vertical 9:16 (phone upright) | **For:** Facebook Reels / Ads
**Concept:** You record your voice → PawMelody turns it into a song with your pet's name → It plays on your TV at home → Your pet hears you and relaxes.

---

## YOU NEED ONLY THIS

- Your phone (this is your camera — clean the lens, charge to 100%)
- The PawMelody app installed + your TV on (Android TV / Fire TV)
- Your pet + their bed/cushion in front of the TV
- Treats (to guide your pet, keep them happy)
- Daylight: film facing a window, never with the window behind you

**Before you press record:**
1. In PawMelody, already create your "My Voice" and generate one song with your pet's real name. Do this BEFORE filming so you never wait on camera.
2. Put your pet's bed in front of the TV. TV volume at normal level.
3. Phone settings: Video 1080p, 30fps. Vertical. Airplane mode ON (no calls ruining a take).

---

## THE SCRIPT — Shoot it shot by shot

Film each shot separately. 3 takes each. Keep the phone STEADY — lean it on books if you have no tripod.

### SHOT 1 — The Hook (0–4s) — Film this LAST, use your best take first
- **Camera:** Prop phone at your pet's height, close to their face.
- **Do:** Your pet sits by the front door / looks at the camera.
- **You say (off-camera, soft):** Nothing. Let it be silent.
- **Text on screen (add later):** `I have to leave him alone all day... 😢`
- 💡 *Tip: Hold a treat next to the phone lens — your pet will look straight into the camera. That look is what stops people scrolling.*

### SHOT 2 — You, talking to camera (4–10s)
- **Camera:** Phone on a shelf/books at eye level, arm's length. You on the sofa, pet beside you if possible.
- **You say (natural, like telling a friend — smile):**
  > "So I tried something. I recorded my voice in this app, PawMelody..."
- **Text on screen:** `I recorded MY voice 🎙️`
- 💡 *Don't read it like an ad. Say it twice fast, once slow. Use the slow, warm one.*

### SHOT 3 — Record your voice (10–16s) — THE DEMO
- **Camera:** Film over your shoulder, phone screen visible. Or screen-record this part and insert it in editing (easier + cleaner — recommended).
- **Do:** Open PawMelody → My Voice → tap Record.
- **You say into the app (exactly this, soft and loving — use your pet's real name):**
  > "Hey [Buddy], it's me. You're safe, I love you, I'll be home soon..."
  Then hum gently for 3 seconds: "hmm hmm hmm..."
- **Text on screen:** `Just 10 seconds of my voice`
- 💡 *Film this shot in a quiet room, no TV/radio behind you. Your voice must be clean.*

### SHOT 4 — The song is created (16–21s)
- **Camera:** Screen recording only (no camera needed).
- **Do:** Show: choosing your pet → choosing "Home Alone" → the song appearing with your pet's name → tap **Play on TV**.
- **You say (voiceover, record this after, in a quiet room):**
  > "...and it turned my voice into a song — with his name in it."
- **Text on screen:** `It became a song — with HIS name ✨`
- 💡 *Speed this up 2× in editing. Nobody watches loading screens.*

### SHOT 5 — The TV moment + pet reaction (21–30s) — THE MONEY SHOT ⭐
- **Camera:** Phone low, wide shot: TV + pet bed in one frame. Press record and WAIT. Film 2–3 minutes non-stop.
- **Do:** From your phone, play the song on the TV. Your voice-song fills the room.
- **Wait for:** ears up → head tilt → pet walks to bed → lies down → sigh / eyes close. That sequence is gold. Do NOT call your pet or direct them. Just let it happen. Treats only if they get bored.
- **You say:** Nothing live. Add this voiceover later:
  > "Now he hears me singing to him at home. Even when I'm not there."
- **Text on screen:** `He hears MY voice on the TV 📺💛` then `Calm. Safe. Not alone.`
- 💡 *This shot sells the app. If the reaction is weak today, film it again tomorrow at their sleepy time (evening works best). One real head-tilt beats everything.*

### SHOT 6 — End card (30–35s)
- **Camera:** Same wide shot, pet settled/asleep. Hold 5 seconds.
- **You say (voiceover):**
  > "PawMelody. Your voice. Their calm."
- **Text on screen (add in editing, big and centered):**
  ```
  🐾 PawMelody
  Your voice. Their calm.
  Try it free
  ```
- 💡 *Freeze the last frame of your sleeping pet for 2 seconds under the text.*

## EXACT WORDS — Full voiceover in one go (if you prefer one clean take)

Record this in your quietest room (a wardrobe full of clothes works great), phone 20cm from your mouth:

> "I have to leave him alone all day. So I recorded my voice in PawMelody, and it turned it into a song with his name in it. Now he hears me singing to him at home — even when I'm not there. PawMelody. Your voice. Their calm."

For a cat, change "him/his" → your cat's name + "her/him". Use the real name everywhere you can — real names build trust.

## NO-FACE VERSION (if you don't want to be on camera)

Same video, zero face time. Replace Shot 2 with:
- **Shot 2B:** Close-up of your HANDS holding the phone, you speaking into it. Text: `I recorded my voice 🎙️` + the same voiceover line.
You stay anonymous, the pet is the star. This version also works great as an ad.

---

## EDIT IT IN CAPCUT (free, 15 minutes)

1. New project → add Shots 1–6 in order → trim each to the times above.
2. Add your screen recording in Shots 3–4 (crop to fill screen vertical).
3. Text → Auto Captions → Create → style: bold white, black outline. Fix your pet's name spelling.
4. Add the big text cards from the script (Text → Add Text). Keep max 5 words per card.
5. Audio: your generated PawMelody song UNDER everything from Shot 4 to the end. Volume: voice 100%, song 25% under speech, 60% in Shot 5.
6. Add the end card (Shot 6 text + logo if you have it).
7. Export: 1080×1920, MP4, 30fps. Name it: `MyVoice_HomeVideo_35s_9x16.mp4`
